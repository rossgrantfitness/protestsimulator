# Protest Simulator (web prototype)

**Play:** https://rossgrantfitness.github.io/protestsimulator/

**Version:** v0.11.0, Unhinged (13 Oct 2026). Main site: https://protestsimulator.com (this GitHub copy is the backup). What's new: https://protestsimulator.com/updates/

A greybox prototype of a crowd-physics comedy: thousands of people marching through a fictional city ("Anywhere, USA"). Push through the crowd, pick things up, start trouble, get filmed. Everyone remembers who threw it.

## Controls

**Phone:** left thumb anywhere on the left moves you (push past the ring to run); drag anywhere else to look. Buttons: PUNCH (hold and drag to aim a throw), SHOVE, KICK, JUMP, GRAB (tap: pick up, hold: grab someone), LOCK; SLO-MO, PHONE and ? at the top.

**Desktop:** WASD move, Shift run, mouse look (click to capture), left click punch / throw, right click shove, F kick, E pick up (hold to grab someone), Q lock on, Space jump/climb, T slow-mo, V phone-video look.

Prototype: placeholder art, sounds and slogans. Built with Unity.
